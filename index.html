What's this place, anyway?

Sync Tank is the whole project: connected aquariums, cameras, inspection tools, spatial maps, and observations. Two tank nodes are our starting point, with room to grow.

EYES IN THE WATER
Floaters provide fixed views. Reels reach small spaces. Reeflex and Raydar explore motorized inspection with limits and stop controls.
SEE SEA TV
The display counter: browse camera perspectives and connect each view to its real tank and position. One part of the larger system.
SIGHTINGS
Save an image with its tank, camera, and time. Local motion analysis helps notice activity. Optional AI field notes are requested by a person.
